## Supplied device frame

All LMT Go cover phones, walkthrough screens, and mobile simulators use the user-supplied `images/device.avif` frame (964 × 1984). The original frame asset is used unchanged as a CSS background, with real interface captures and the interactive iframe fitted inside its display. The screen aperture follows the supplied asset bounds (23px from each side and 16px from the top/bottom), with one rounded clip shared by the status area and interface. This replaces the earlier CSS-drawn hardware. Redesigned screen captures use a 390 × 800 viewport to match the frame's display proportions.

`metro-storyboard-v2.png` was generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for this concept page. It is an illustrated scenario, not a record of observed user research. The original generated output is preserved under the Codex generated_images directory.

The other `metro-*.png` screen previews are unedited browser captures of Asith's actual Metro redesign prototype. The `original-*.png` files are the supplied original-app references. Personal profile and wallet QR screenshots were not copied into the public assets.

## Storyboard prompt

Create a hand-drawn UX storyboard illustration for a Sri Lankan public bus commute redesign case study. Wide landscape image, roughly 3:1 ratio, four equally sized scenes in ONE horizontal row separated by very faint uneven pencil panel rules. No words, no captions, no numbers, no logos, no UI text. Medium: genuine soft graphite pencil on clean warm-white sketchbook paper, visible exploratory construction lines, fine delicate crosshatching, light smudges, slightly imperfect freehand strokes, professional product designer's observational field sketches. Same adult Sri Lankan woman commuter with shoulder-length hair and a small shoulder bag across all four panels. Panel 1: at home near a window in early morning, checking her phone before leaving, medium shot. Panel 2: standing at a modest Colombo roadside bus stop looking at her phone, simple bus approaching in background, enough context for a bus shelter and tropical roadside tree. Panel 3: side view boarding a single-decker city bus, holding phone toward a small boarding scanner near driver, not a train. Panel 4: seated on the bus by a window with a relaxed expression, phone in hand and bag in lap, glimpse of trees outside. Keep every scene visually simple with generous breathing room and a similar mid-distance viewpoint, human gestures must read clearly at small sizes. Minimal muted red coloured-pencil accent only on a tiny bus stripe and the commuter's bag in each panel, everything else graphite. No photorealism, no 3D, no polished digital vector art, no dense shading, no watercolor, no decorative arrows. Image must feel like a thoughtful pencil storyboard on paper, not an advertising illustration.

## Bus-reference revision

The final storyboard was edited with the built-in ImageGen tool using the user's exterior and interior bus photographs. The revision preserved the four-scene composition, the commuter, and graphite sketch style while matching the modern Foton Metro bus: broad dark front windscreen, red front and bumper, white sides with blue/teal trim, purple fabric seats, silver rails, hanging straps, and yellow boarding-reader housing. No photographs or identifiable people from the bus-reference images were inserted.

### Cover background prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create an understated contemporary editorial graphic illustration for the BACKGROUND of a Sri Lankan Lanka Metro Transit app redesign portfolio cover. Landscape canvas, approximately 3:2. Very light cool ivory / mist gray background with a quiet low-contrast city street scene, refined hand-drawn ink outlines, softly filled geometric shapes and subtle paper grain. Draw an adult Sri Lankan woman commuter with dark shoulder-length hair, casual blouse and trousers and a small shoulder bag, walking naturally toward the open front door of a modern single-deck city bus. Keep this scene along the LOWER THIRD of the composition: woman left of center, bus extending from the lower right edge. The bus must resemble the actual modern Sri Lankan Foton Metro buses: long white sides, very broad tall dark front windscreen, red vertical front corners and red lower front bumper, pale blue and teal sweeping front trim, large rectangular side windows, silver grab rails visible near the door. This is a low-floor urban transit bus, not an old rounded bus or a luxury coach. Use muted dusty Metro red, soft blue/teal and charcoal ink, with very restrained saturation. Suggest a bus-stop pole, minimal roadside tree silhouettes and pavement lines as faint background details. Keep the UPPER HALF and CENTRE predominantly empty and very pale, because an exact existing Metro logo and real app screenshots will be layered in separately. The commuter and bus should feel like quiet supporting background graphics rather than a hero photograph. No words, text, numbers, logos, UI, phones, devices, mockups, watermarks, frames or captions. No photorealism, 3D, gradients, clip-art stock icons, or children's cartoon style. Calm polished adult editorial illustration with confident fine lines and ample negative space.
